A feed reader that puts your subscriptions back in the order you chose, not the order an algorithm chose.

Subscriptions on the left, the timeline in the middle, the article on the right — on a phone the same three panes become three taps.

WHAT YOU GET

• RSS, Atom, RDF and JSON Feed, all handled
• Smart feeds: Today, All Unread, Starred, All Articles
• Folders, with unread counts, and a marker on any feed that stopped responding
• Full-text search across every article you have, matching as you type
• Star what you want to keep, mark what you have read — and refreshing never undoes either
• Import and export OPML, with folders intact, so moving in and out is a single file

NO ACCOUNT, NO SYNC SERVICE

Day Sheets talks directly to the sites you subscribe to. There is no account, no server of ours in the middle, and nothing collected about what you read.

Open source, built with the Day framework: one Rust codebase drawing itself with each platform's real native widgets. github.com/daybrite/Day-Sheets
